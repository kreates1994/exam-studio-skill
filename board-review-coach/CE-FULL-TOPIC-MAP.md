# Civil Engineering Licensure Exam — Full Topic Map (Chevk Board Review Coach)

**Scope and date:** Philippines, reviewed 20 October 2026. This is an instructional breakdown of the publicly released competencies, **not** a claim to know every actual PRC item or a prediction of questions. The official competency identifiers below track the PRC's *2022 Table of Specifications, Annex A*, which is the exam item-bank specification, and the *March 2026 revised program* for section weights and listed subjects.

- [PRC March 2026 revised CE exam program](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/RevisedExamProgramMarch2026CivilEngg.pdf)
- [PRC 2026 subject order notice](https://prc.gov.ph/article/adjustments-order-subjects-be-administered-starting-march-2026-cele)

## Coverage guarantees and limits

- All **113 named CE TOS competencies** below must be selectable, independently teachable, independently assessed, and counted in coverage reporting (PSAD 25 + HGE 13 + MSTE 75).
- Text following each competency is a Chevk **suggested practice subtopic breakdown**, not an exhaustive official list or official allocation of items.
- A learner can select a subject, category, competency ID, or nested subtopic; maintain exact parent competency attribution.
- Track statuses per competency: `not_assessed`, `studied`, `practiced`, `needs_review`, `demonstrated`. One exposure must not imply demonstrated competence. Report official competency coverage separately from subtopic breadth and scores. Never call either a PRC pass prediction.
- If a learner says "all topics", show the full current track syllabus in sensible pages, and offer a topic-by-topic route without hiding a category.
- A mock follows the current official **section weighting of PSAD 35%, MSTE 35%, HGE 30%**. Within subjects use the official per-competency shares when a full-length subject mock or reasonably large mock allows; tiny mocks disclose partial coverage.
- Source/edition-dependent numerical code rules, structural strength reduction factors, limits, soil/bearing correlations, drainage rules and legal provisions require a user-supplied or verified current source edition. Otherwise teach concepts without asserting an unverified value.
- Refresh this map if PRC publishes a newer TOS or exam program. New programs may list broader areas that remain nested within older TOS competencies; cross-index them rather than silently omitting them.

## PSAD — Principles of Structural Analysis and Design (35% of CE overall)

### 1. Engineering Mechanics

- **PSAD 1.1 — Static-equilibrium reactions.** Practice coverage: Free-body diagrams; 2D force and moment balance; distributed/resultant loads; support reactions; cables and pulleys.
- **PSAD 1.2 — Friction at equilibrium.** Practice coverage: Static/limiting friction; impending motion; wedges; blocks on inclines; belt-friction extensions.
- **PSAD 1.3 — Non-coplanar force-system reactions.** Practice coverage: 3D vectors and moments; equilibrium in three dimensions; space structures; equivalent force-couple systems.

### 2. Strength of Materials

- **PSAD 2.1 — Material properties and load capacity.** Practice coverage: Stress–strain response; modulus, yield and ultimate strength; ductility, toughness and brittle failure; factor of safety.
- **PSAD 2.2 — Stress from axial loading and flexure.** Practice coverage: Direct/combined axial stress; flexural normal stress; transformed sections; principal axes; eccentric loading.
- **PSAD 2.3 — Elastic deformation and resulting stresses.** Practice coverage: Axial deformation; thermal strain; compatibility; torsion and shear stress; Mohr's circle; principal and maximum shear stresses; strain energy.

### 3. Theory of Structures

- **PSAD 3.1 — Maximum reactions and deflections.** Practice coverage: Beam and frame reaction analysis; elastic-curve formulas; double integration; moment-area and conjugate-beam methods.
- **PSAD 3.2 — Influence lines and moving loads.** Practice coverage: Influence ordinates for reactions, shear and moment; vehicle and concentrated moving loads; maximum/minimum effects.
- **PSAD 3.3 — Member shear, moment, and torsion.** Practice coverage: Section cuts; shear-force and bending-moment diagrams; internal axial and torsional loading.
- **PSAD 3.4 — Structural indeterminacy.** Practice coverage: Static versus kinematic indeterminacy; stability; restraints; beams, frames, and planar trusses.
- **PSAD 3.5 — Pattern-loading effects on reactions.** Practice coverage: Continuous beams and frames; live-load placement; patterned and unpatterned loading.
- **PSAD 3.6 — Deflection, flexure, shear, and torsion analysis.** Practice coverage: Truss member forces; method of joints/sections; virtual work; force and displacement methods; compatibility; stiffness concepts.

### 4. Reinforced and Prestressed Concrete

- **PSAD 4.1 — Concrete-beam load capacity.** Practice coverage: Singly and doubly reinforced beams; strain compatibility; neutral axis; flexural strength; T- and flanged beams.
- **PSAD 4.2 — Beam reinforcement for flexure, shear, and torsion.** Practice coverage: Flexural steel areas; stirrup design; development/anchorage principles; shear-torsion interactions; code-edition-dependent limits.
- **PSAD 4.3 — Concrete-column load capacity.** Practice coverage: Short/slender columns; axial and combined axial-flexure loads; tied and spiraled columns; interaction diagrams.
- **PSAD 4.4 — Column flexural, shear, and torsional reinforcement.** Practice coverage: Longitudinal reinforcement and transverse ties; eccentric columns; shear and confinement provisions when source edition verified.
- **PSAD 4.5 — Effects of prestressing.** Practice coverage: Prestress force/eccentricity; top and bottom stresses; losses; load balancing; transfer versus service stresses; tendon profile.

### 5. Steel Beams and Columns

- **PSAD 5.1 — Steel-beam capacity.** Practice coverage: Elastic/plastic flexure; section modulus; lateral-torsional buckling; shear capacity; serviceability.
- **PSAD 5.2 — Steel-column capacity.** Practice coverage: Slenderness and effective length; Euler buckling; column curves; axial compression and buckling.
- **PSAD 5.3 — Eccentrically loaded steel members.** Practice coverage: Combined axial-flexure stress; unsymmetrical bending; interaction and stability considerations.

### 6. Joints and Connections

- **PSAD 6.1 — Bolt forces and stresses.** Practice coverage: Direct shear and tension; eccentric bolt groups; bearing; bolt spacing and connection capacity with verified code rules.
- **PSAD 6.2 — Weld forces and stresses.** Practice coverage: Fillet-weld groups; effective throat; direct and eccentric loading; polar-moment approach.

### 7. Footings and Foundations

- **PSAD 7.1 — Spread-footing design.** Practice coverage: Isolated and combined footings; soil-pressure distribution; one-way and punching shear; flexural reinforcement; eccentric reactions.
- **PSAD 7.2 — Loads carried by piled foundations.** Practice coverage: Pile groups and cap reactions; eccentric loading of groups; pile-load distribution; settlement and lateral response concepts.

### 8. Construction Materials Testing

- **PSAD 8.1 — Testing apparatus and concrete mixes.** Practice coverage: Aggregate tests; slump and compressive strength; cement, steel and concrete sample testing; mixture proportions; QA/QC.

## HGE — Hydraulics and Principles of Geotechnical Engineering (30% of CE overall)

### 1. Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics

- **HGE 1.1 — Fluid properties.** Practice coverage: Density; specific gravity; unit weight; viscosity; surface tension; compressibility; vapor pressure.
- **HGE 1.2 — Hydrostatic pressure and center of pressure.** Practice coverage: Pressure variation; manometers; plane and curved surfaces; resultant hydrostatic force; center of pressure; dams and gates; relatively accelerated/rotating liquids.
- **HGE 1.3 — Fluid flow.** Practice coverage: Continuity; Bernoulli and energy equation; laminar/turbulent flow; Reynolds number; pipes, major/minor head loss, series/parallel networks; open-channel flow; Manning; orifices/weirs.
- **HGE 1.4 — Buoyancy and flotation.** Practice coverage: Archimedes' principle; immersed and floating bodies; equilibrium; center of buoyancy; metacentric height and stability.
- **HGE 1.5 — Hydrodynamics.** Practice coverage: Linear momentum equation; forces on bends, jets and nozzles; impulse-momentum; impact of jets; hydraulic power.
- **HGE 1.6 — Water supply.** Practice coverage: Flow demand; reservoirs/tanks; pipe networks; pumping and pump head; municipal/building distribution fundamentals; water-system losses.

### 2. Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering

- **HGE 2.1 — Soil properties.** Practice coverage: Phase relationships; water content; void ratio; porosity; unit weight; relative density; Atterberg limits; compaction parameters.
- **HGE 2.2 — Soil classification.** Practice coverage: Particle size; gradation curves; USCS/AASHTO fundamentals; plasticity chart; soil identification.
- **HGE 2.3 — Permeability and seepage.** Practice coverage: Darcy's law; hydraulic gradient; seepage through stratified soils; flow nets; uplift and piping.
- **HGE 2.4 — Stresses in soil mass.** Practice coverage: Total/effective stress; pore pressures; Boussinesq stress increments; earth pressure; overburden; stresses under foundations.
- **HGE 2.5 — Soil strength.** Practice coverage: Mohr–Coulomb shear strength; direct-shear/triaxial concepts; undrained and drained conditions; slope-stability basics.
- **HGE 2.6 — Settlement.** Practice coverage: Consolidation theory; normally/over-consolidated clays; immediate and primary settlement; rate of consolidation; compaction effects.
- **HGE 2.7 — Foundation engineering.** Practice coverage: Bearing-capacity methods and factors; shallow/deep foundations; factor of safety; retaining walls, lateral earth pressures and slope-stability applications.

## MSTE — Applied Mathematics, Surveying, Transportation, and Construction (35% of CE overall)

### 1. Calculus

- **MSTE 1.1 — Derivatives and applications.** Practice coverage: Limits, derivative rules, rates, extrema, related rates, tangent lines and optimization.
- **MSTE 1.2 — Integration and definite integrals.** Practice coverage: Antiderivatives, substitution, integration by parts, areas, accumulations and numerical checks.
- **MSTE 1.3 — Plane curves and hyperbolic functions.** Practice coverage: Cartesian, polar, parametric curves; curve geometry; hyperbolic-function identities.
- **MSTE 1.4 — Sequences and series.** Practice coverage: Infinite sequences; convergence; Taylor/Maclaurin and power series; approximations.
- **MSTE 1.5 — Partial derivatives and differential equations.** Practice coverage: Multivariable differentiation; gradients; basic PDE/ODE formulation.

### 2. Differential Equations

- **MSTE 2.1 — Applications of integration.** Practice coverage: Areas, volumes of revolution, centroids, work and force from varying loads.
- **MSTE 2.2 — First-order differential equations.** Practice coverage: Separable, linear, exact and initial-value ODEs; engineering applications.
- **MSTE 2.3 — Systems of linear differential equations.** Practice coverage: Coupled linear ODEs; matrix/state formulations; mechanical and physical systems.
- **MSTE 2.4 — Double and triple integrals.** Practice coverage: Physical meaning; mass, area/volume and distributed properties; coordinate transforms.
- **MSTE 2.5 — Laplace-transform solutions.** Practice coverage: Laplace transform tables; initial-value problems; partial fractions; inverse transforms.

### 3. Engineering Data Analysis

- **MSTE 3.1 — Data description.** Practice coverage: Descriptive statistics; mean, median, modes, dispersion, variance, standard deviation and data visualization.
- **MSTE 3.2 — Probability distributions.** Practice coverage: Discrete/continuous random variables; binomial, Poisson and normal distributions; probability calculations.
- **MSTE 3.3 — Parameter estimation.** Practice coverage: Samples; confidence intervals; sampling distributions; simple hypothesis tests.
- **MSTE 3.4 — Linear regression.** Practice coverage: Least-squares line; correlation; interpreting slope, residuals, prediction.
- **MSTE 3.5 — Stochastic processes and queues.** Practice coverage: Poisson processes; queues and arrival/service rates; basic queue measures.

### 4. Numerical Methods

- **MSTE 4.1 — Linear algebra and calculus fundamentals.** Practice coverage: Vectors/matrices; derivative/integral interpretations; numerical error.
- **MSTE 4.2 — Systems of linear equations.** Practice coverage: Gaussian elimination; determinants; matrix inverses; linear system solutions.
- **MSTE 4.3 — Curve fitting and interpolation.** Practice coverage: Newton/Lagrange interpolation; least-squares fitting; engineering data tables.
- **MSTE 4.4 — Numerical differentiation and integration.** Practice coverage: Finite differences; trapezoidal/Simpson rules; error assessment.
- **MSTE 4.5 — Numerical ODEs and boundary-value problems.** Practice coverage: Euler and Runge–Kutta concepts; iterative boundary-value techniques.

### 5. Physics for Engineers

- **MSTE 5.1 — Newton's laws.** Practice coverage: Translational motion; force systems; F=ma; free-body diagrams.
- **MSTE 5.2 — Work and Hooke's law.** Practice coverage: Work from constant/variable forces; springs; mechanical work.
- **MSTE 5.3 — Energy conservation.** Practice coverage: Potential and kinetic energies; work-energy principle; conservative forces.
- **MSTE 5.4 — Linear/angular momentum.** Practice coverage: Impulse, momentum, collisions, rotational dynamics.
- **MSTE 5.5 — Calculus-based statics and kinematics.** Practice coverage: Particle/rigid-body motion; acceleration relations; related derivatives/integrals.

### 6. Engineering Economics

- **MSTE 6.1 — Compounding and equivalence.** Practice coverage: Simple/compound interest; nominal/effective rates; cash-flow diagrams; P/F/A factors.
- **MSTE 6.2 — NPV, return, payback, B/C ratio.** Practice coverage: Present worth; IRR/ROR; payback; benefit-cost analysis.
- **MSTE 6.3 — Investment alternatives.** Practice coverage: Incremental comparison; equivalent annual worth; life-cycle costs.
- **MSTE 6.4 — Equipment replacement and retirement.** Practice coverage: Replacement age; economic service life; salvage; ownership/operating cost.
- **MSTE 6.5 — Depreciation and taxes.** Practice coverage: Straight-line, declining-balance and sum-of-years methods; after-tax cash-flow basics.

### 7. Construction Surveying and Layout

- **MSTE 7.1 — Survey practice and measurements.** Practice coverage: Angles; distances; leveling; errors; surveying equipment and field procedure.
- **MSTE 7.2 — Traverses.** Practice coverage: Latitudes/departures; closure; azimuth/bearing; area; Bowditch/transit adjustments.
- **MSTE 7.3 — Horizontal and vertical curves.** Practice coverage: Simple/compound/reverse curves; stationing; degree of curve; vertical parabolic curves.
- **MSTE 7.4 — Layout techniques.** Practice coverage: Construction setting-out; elevations and benchmarks; horizontal/vertical control.
- **MSTE 7.5 — Construction control.** Practice coverage: Control networks; as-built checks; tolerances; quality-control methodology.

### 8. Construction Materials

- **MSTE 8.1 — Concrete and supporting systems.** Practice coverage: Cement/aggregates; admixtures; reinforcement; formworks; scaffolding.
- **MSTE 8.2 — Masonry, mortar, grout and erection.** Practice coverage: Concrete masonry; masonry quantities and erection methods; mortars/grouts.
- **MSTE 8.3 — Concrete production and placement.** Practice coverage: Mixing; handling; conveying; placing; vibrating/consolidation and curing.
- **MSTE 8.4 — Iron and steel construction.** Practice coverage: Steel properties; detailing and erection; connections and corrosion basics.
- **MSTE 8.5 — Timber and engineered panels.** Practice coverage: Lumber grading; plywood; glulam; moisture and usage selection.

### 9. Highway Engineering

- **MSTE 9.1 — Highway policies and administration.** Practice coverage: Highway planning concepts; classification and agencies; design governance.
- **MSTE 9.2 — Geometric highway design.** Practice coverage: Sight distances; superelevation; grades; cross sections; design standards (verify edition).
- **MSTE 9.3 — Route location.** Practice coverage: Alignment selection; surveys; horizontal/vertical route constraints.
- **MSTE 9.4 — Pavement design and construction.** Practice coverage: Flexible/rigid pavements; subgrade; pavement layers; construction sequence.
- **MSTE 9.5 — Highway drainage and earthworks.** Practice coverage: Roadside drainage; subgrades; embankments; cut slopes; road foundations.

### 10. Construction Occupational Safety and Health

- **MSTE 10.1 — Injury accidents and costs.** Practice coverage: Construction hazards; causes, prevention and human/economic costs.
- **MSTE 10.2 — RA 11058 and OSH rules.** Practice coverage: Statutory safety framework; obligations, standards and amendments subject to source checking.
- **MSTE 10.3 — Construction health-and-safety programs.** Practice coverage: Risk assessment; PPE; safety officers; toolbox talks; incident controls.
- **MSTE 10.4 — Safety on contracts and records.** Practice coverage: Contract responsibility; logs; culture; workforce safety and compliance.
- **MSTE 10.5 — Management-led project safety.** Practice coverage: Company safety policy; project coordination; contractor management.

### 11. Transportation Engineering

- **MSTE 11.1 — Urban transportation planning.** Practice coverage: Travel demand; traffic generation; forecasting and land-use interactions.
- **MSTE 11.2 — Transportation network analysis.** Practice coverage: Shortest paths; assignment concepts; network flow; routing.
- **MSTE 11.3 — Quantitative transport methods.** Practice coverage: Flow, density, speed; capacity; queuing; traffic stream relationships.
- **MSTE 11.4 — Intermodal systems.** Practice coverage: Modal choices; transfer terminals; system performance and evaluation.
- **MSTE 11.5 — Construction cost estimating overlap.** Practice coverage: The TOS includes building, bridge and construction cost estimates here; also cross-indexed to quantity surveying.

### 12. Quantity Surveying

- **MSTE 12.1 — Preparing construction cost estimates.** Practice coverage: Buildings/bridges; preliminary and detailed estimation approaches.
- **MSTE 12.2 — Specifications and plans.** Practice coverage: Reading plans, details, notes and bills of quantities.
- **MSTE 12.3 — Labor/material/equipment unit costs.** Practice coverage: Unit-rate build-ups; waste; productivity; indirect costs.
- **MSTE 12.4 — Detailed quantity takeoff.** Practice coverage: Earthworks; concrete; formwork; reinforcement; roads; measurement conventions.
- **MSTE 12.5 — Total-project cost summaries.** Practice coverage: Direct/indirect costs; overhead; contingencies; schedules of quantities.

### 13. Construction Principles and Management

- **MSTE 13.1 — General design requirements.** Practice coverage: Project design principles and constructability; governing requirements.
- **MSTE 13.2 — General conditions of contracts.** Practice coverage: Contract terms, roles, variations and claims principles.
- **MSTE 13.3 — Labor/material quantity estimating.** Practice coverage: Crew productivity; resource needs; quantity and costing calculations.
- **MSTE 13.4 — Organization, cost control and records.** Practice coverage: Schedules; project teams; documentation; earned-value and CPM/PERT as supporting methods.

### 14. Construction Trades and Specialties

- **MSTE 14.2 — Plastics in construction.** Practice coverage: PVC and other plastics; material selection and applications.

## Learning system — mandatory topic-level behavior

1. **BROWSE TOPICS [subject]**: list official categories and count, then offer the numbered competencies; do not overwhelm the first screen by dumping all 113 rows unless asked.
2. **STUDY [code or name]**: explain assumptions and units; identify the PRC competency; give prerequisite intuition, formula and interpretation, worked engineering example, a verified fresh problem and mistakes/traps.
3. **QUIZ [code] [n]**: distribute problem types across its named practice subtopics; include conceptual, numerical and reading/interpretation items as appropriate.
4. **MOCK [n] / MOCK FULL**: weighted sampling of subjects and then competencies; do not say "complete syllabus tested" when n is too small to cover it. Distinguish item generation from a vetted fixed bank.
5. **COVERAGE**: show unassessed, studied, practiced, needs-review and demonstrated counts for each official subject, and list gaps; a topic moves to `demonstrated` only with repeated unassisted independently checked attempts, never by reading.
6. **WEAK TOPICS**: rank objectively by independent attempts and errors while retaining "unassessed" where there is no evidence.
7. **CURRENT CODE**: ask for or verify edition and the actual section/table before giving exact limits or a regulation-specific graded item.
8. **PREPARATION ORDER**: first sample each PRC category, then target deficiencies while leaving scheduled breadth reviews.

## Supplemental foundations

Basic arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, unit conversions, scientific-calculator techniques, free-body diagrams, construction drawing interpretation and exam pacing are **supporting prerequisites**, not additional weighted official CE subjects. Teach them on demand and attribute graded questions to the correct governing TOS competency when applicable.
