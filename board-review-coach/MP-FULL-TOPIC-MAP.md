# Master Plumber Licensure Exam — Full Topic Map (Chevk Board Review Coach)

**Scope and date:** Philippines, reviewed 20 October 2026. The four tested subject weights follow the publicly released February 2026 PRC Master Plumber examination program and the 2024 implementing regulations. The following **42 syllabus headings** are derived from the PRC's published **1995 Master Plumber syllabus**, which is publicly accessible but old. The learner's target exam program and currently applicable National Plumbing Code edition must still be checked. This is a learning syllabus, **not** a guaranteed list of every possible board question.

Primary sources:
- [PRC Master Plumber syllabus (1995)](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/Board%20of%20Master%20Plumber%20-%20Syllabi_0.pdf)
- [PRC February 2026 Master Plumber examination program](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/RA_FEBRUARY%202026%20MASTER%20PLUMBERS%20LE_RO1_ROSALES.pdf)
- [PRC Master Plumber 2024 IRR, subjects and weights](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2024-03%20published-%20Rules%20and%20Regulations%20of%20R.A.%201378%20master%20plumber.pdf)

The letter identifiers below mirror the four-section official 1995 syllabus headings; Chevk's stable topic IDs add a section prefix to prevent collisions. Text after each heading is an **illustrative teaching expansion**, not a certified PRC item distribution.

## MP-ARITH — Plumbing Arithmetic (10%)

- **MP-ARITH-A: Modern algebra.** Learning and problem coverage: Real numbers, proportions, exponentials, functions and graphs, simultaneous equations, pipe-flow unit arithmetic.
- **MP-ARITH-B: Trigonometry.** Learning and problem coverage: Trigonometric functions, triangles, vectors, angle relationships, offsets, slopes and layout.
- **MP-ARITH-C: Geometry.** Learning and problem coverage: Coordinate and solid geometry; circles, pipes, tanks, surfaces, volumes, dimensions, conic sections.
- **MP-ARITH-D: Physics.** Learning and problem coverage: Units, forces, motion, fluids, pressure, gases, energy, temperature, heat expansion and phase changes.
- **MP-ARITH-E: Financial arithmetic.** Learning and problem coverage: Simple and compound interest, discounts, annuities, amortization, sinking funds, depreciation and costs.

## MP-CODE — Plumbing Code (10%)

- **MP-CODE-A: Code of ethics.** Learning and problem coverage: Ethical obligations, professional responsibility, duties to clients and the public, licensed practice.
- **MP-CODE-B: Permits.** Learning and problem coverage: Applications, inspection processes, documentation, responsibilities and applicable local requirements.
- **MP-CODE-C: Plumbing terminology.** Learning and problem coverage: Definitions for pipe, fitting, fixture, trap, drain, vent, stack, sewer and plumbing system terms.
- **MP-CODE-D: General regulations.** Learning and problem coverage: Alignment, pipe slope, installation standards, workmanship, prohibited connections and pipe protection.
- **MP-CODE-E: Material quality and weight.** Learning and problem coverage: Pipe materials and classes, relevant ratings, material quality standards and identification.
- **MP-CODE-F: Joints and connections.** Learning and problem coverage: Joining methods for different pipe materials; limitations and inspections.
- **MP-CODE-G: Traps and cleanouts.** Learning and problem coverage: Trap types, trap seals, protection, cleanout placement and sizing principles.
- **MP-CODE-H: Water supply and distribution.** Learning and problem coverage: Potable supply, piping arrangements, storage and backflow prevention principles.
- **MP-CODE-I: Plumbing fixtures.** Learning and problem coverage: Fixture classes, connections, location and typical fixture requirements.
- **MP-CODE-J: Ventilation of rooms and fixtures.** Learning and problem coverage: Ventilation rules for fixture rooms and associated requirements.
- **MP-CODE-K: Trap/drain sizes and fixture-unit values.** Learning and problem coverage: Reading the approved fixture unit and minimum drainage/trap size tables; do not guess values.
- **MP-CODE-L: Maximum fixture units on stacks.** Learning and problem coverage: Soil/waste stack sizing and connections; using approved stack-capacity tables.
- **MP-CODE-M: Permissible vent sizes and heights.** Learning and problem coverage: Stack vent, vent sizing and routing; verify actual code-edition tables before numeric answers.
- **MP-CODE-N: Septic vault construction.** Learning and problem coverage: Septic vault/tank general provisions, watertightness, siting and disposal systems.
- **MP-CODE-O: Hot-water and steam waste.** Learning and problem coverage: Hot water, temperature and discharge protection; special waste requirements.
- **MP-CODE-P: House drain and house sewer.** Learning and problem coverage: Drain/sewer connections, slope, sizing and cleanouts using verified rules.
- **MP-CODE-Q: Horizontal branches.** Learning and problem coverage: Branch routing, slope, sizing, connections and fixture load.
- **MP-CODE-R: Special waste.** Learning and problem coverage: Industrial/chemical/special discharges, pretreatment and allowable arrangements.
- **MP-CODE-S: Inspection and testing.** Learning and problem coverage: Rough-in/final inspection, pressure/leak tests and approved testing procedures.
- **MP-CODE-T: Special provisions.** Learning and problem coverage: Additional code applications; record source edition and section.

## MP-SAN — Sanitation, Plumbing Design and Installation (40%)

- **MP-SAN-A: Development of rural water sources.** Learning and problem coverage: Water source selection; needs and quality; groundwater/surface water protection and contamination safeguards.
- **MP-SAN-B: Wastewater treatment and disposal.** Learning and problem coverage: Septic tanks, private wastewater disposal, effluent, seepage and wastewater treatment basics.
- **MP-SAN-C: Water-supply distribution system.** Learning and problem coverage: Demand, sizing, branch layout, pressure/head, pumps, tanks, valves, fixture supply.
- **MP-SAN-D: Sanitary drainage and venting.** Learning and problem coverage: Soil and waste stacks; branches; traps, cleanouts; vents; house drain/sewer; manholes.
- **MP-SAN-E: Storm and subsurface drainage.** Learning and problem coverage: Roof/floor/area drainage, downspouts, ground drains, subdrains, catch basins and manholes.
- **MP-SAN-F: Water-heating system.** Learning and problem coverage: Heaters, hot-water supply and return piping, circulation and expansion.
- **MP-SAN-G: Dry and wet standpipes.** Learning and problem coverage: Basic dry/wet standpipe systems and fire-protection installation interfaces; check applicable laws/codes.
- **MP-SAN-H: Pumps, tanks, cisterns and sewage pits.** Learning and problem coverage: Pump head and selection, storage sizing, hydropneumatic tanks, scum and sewage pits.
- **MP-SAN-I: Installation and workmanship.** Learning and problem coverage: Pipe laying, hangers/supports, joints, leak testing, material storage and inspection.
- **MP-SAN-J: Plans and specifications.** Learning and problem coverage: Orthographic and isometric drawings; schematic/riser diagrams, details and legends; specifications.

## MP-PRAC — Practical Problems and Experiences (40%)

- **MP-PRAC-A: Plan reading and interpretation.** Learning and problem coverage: Plans, symbols, legends, specifications; isometric, riser and orthographic drawing interpretation.
- **MP-PRAC-B: Plumbing materials.** Learning and problem coverage: Pipe/fittings/valves/regulators: shape, size, quality, ratings, uses and compatibility.
- **MP-PRAC-C: Fixtures and appliances.** Learning and problem coverage: Identify fixtures, equipment, connection needs, installation location/height (verify code).
- **MP-PRAC-D: Hand and machine tools.** Learning and problem coverage: Cutting, threading, reaming, flaring, brazing, welding, drain-cleaning and safety.
- **MP-PRAC-E: Maintenance and repair.** Learning and problem coverage: Fault detection; leaks; blockages; corrosion; pump/valve/fixture maintenance.
- **MP-PRAC-F: Bill of materials and estimates.** Learning and problem coverage: Takeoff, quantities, unit prices, labor and equipment; cost summaries.
- **MP-PRAC-G: Obligations and contracts.** Learning and problem coverage: Plans, contracts, reports, site inspections, procurement and acceptance of workmanship.

## Supporting cross-cutting skills

- Dimensional analysis, arithmetic and unit conversions, metric/imperial interpretation.
- Hydrostatics, pipe pressure, Bernoulli, head losses and pumps **within the appropriate plumbing subject**, without inventing legal code values.
- Quantity takeoffs, fixture-counting, drawings, cost estimating, equipment fault finding and repair.
- Exam-calculator and time-management tactics.

## Mandatory modes and progress behavior

1. **BROWSE TOPICS MP** must allow browsing all four subjects and all **42 headings**. Never imply that seeing a topic proves competence.
2. **STUDY [code]** must provide a concept, a worked situation, relevant equations/units, source verification for code limits, a distinct check problem and common traps.
3. **QUIZ [code]** must mix definitions, plan reading, numerical computation, judgment and interpretation as appropriate to that topic. Never fabricate a code excerpt or numerical minimum.
4. **MOCK [n]** allocates subject counts at **10% / 40% / 10% / 40%** by largest-remainder rounding and diversifies the covered headings inside each subject.
5. **COVERAGE / UNASSESSED** lists topic counts with statuses `not_assessed`, `studied`, `practiced`, `needs_review`, and `demonstrated`, plus the overdue retrieval queue and per-topic independent scores.
6. For **code-size, slope, fixture unit, permitted joint, inspection and legal questions**, use the user-provided current code edition/table or a verified official/authoritative source before answering with a fixed mandatory value; if unavailable, say the exact value is not verified, give method, and offer a non-code-specific practice example.
7. For numeric questions include assumptions, conversions, formulas, unit checks and independent verification.
8. Expand a learner's requested obscure or emerging topic when it is reasonably within plumbing practice but label it **supplementary / not explicitly named in the source syllabus** instead of fabricating official coverage.
9. Full-syllabus status means **all 42 official headings mapped**, not every secret test item covered or mastery proven.
